About

Who are we?

Lawler Collective is a photography, videography and design team based in Macomb, Michigan.

We love shooting people and things with a story behind them. Maybe it's a senior getting ready for what's next, a car someone spent years building, or a spot that looks unreal at golden hour. We want your photos to bring back how that moment felt, not just what it looked like.

We're always trying something new, whether that's a different style of shooting or something beyond photos, like video and design. Tell us what you have in mind and we'll figure out how to make it happen together. Years from now, we want you to look back at these and still love them.

Let's tell your story, one frame at a time.
