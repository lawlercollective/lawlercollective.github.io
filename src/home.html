About

Who are we?

Lawler Collective is a photography, videography, and design team based in Macomb, Michigan.

We photograph people, places, and the things that matter to them. Whether it's a senior preparing for the next chapter, a car built over years of work, or a landscape at golden hour, our goal is to capture how a moment felt, not just how it looked.

We're always refining our craft and exploring new mediums, from fresh photographic styles to videography and design. Share your vision with us, and we'll work with you to bring it to life with images you'll be proud of for years to come.

Let's tell your story, one frame at a time.
